# Changelog

## 0.2.2

- The tile shows the coming days on any tile wide enough: under the weather now on a tall tile, at its right on a
  short one (the name, icon, temperature and word in the left third).

## 0.2.1

- The days get most of the card again, in larger letters; the weather now is one strip above them (temperature,
  feels like, humidity, wind, UV), and the coming hours moved to today's and tomorrow's pages.
- A day's page gives the forecast in words more room and wraps it whole in a smaller font when it is long.
- The hourly forecast is asked for after the daily one has come in, never at the same moment.

## 0.2.0

- The card shows the weather now at the top (temperature, feels like, humidity, wind with its direction, pressure,
  dew point, UV) and the coming hours under it, above the row per day.
- A tap on a day shows it in full: the high and low large, a short forecast in words, the rain, wind, humidity and
  UV, and that day's hours where the hourly forecast reaches.

## 0.1.0

- First version: the Bold forecast tile and the Bold week card, with a day shown in full on a tap.
